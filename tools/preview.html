Holiday heroes, month by month

Each preview is a committed assets/hero-<key>-light|dark.svg with the clock running, as the Worker serves it. Every theme keeps the layout, type and animation; it changes the panel tint, the two background glows, the lattice and the hands. All text meets WCAG AA (4.5:1) in both modes.

What changes per themePanel tint, main glow (behind the clock) and a second glow (lower left), lattice dots as a two-colour gradient, hour hand and hour badge in the main accent, seconds sweep, hub and minute badge ring in the second accent.
How it shipsmake writes hero-<holiday>-light|dark.svg beside the everyday hero, and assets/holidays.json. The profile-clock Worker picks the hero for today's London date, alternating by the hour when holidays overlap, and falls back to the everyday hero.
Moving feastsLunar, lunisolar and astronomical holidays sit in the month they usually fall in; holidays.toml carries their sourced dates. A year with no sourced date is listed under its theme and has no theme that year.
